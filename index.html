Introduction

El Nido is a popular tourist destination in Palawan, Philippines, known for its beautiful limestone cliffs, clear blue waters, white-sand beaches, lagoons, and islands. It is located at the northern part of Palawan and is one of the country's most famous island destinations.

El Nido offers many activities such as island hopping, swimming, snorkeling, kayaking, diving, and sightseeing. Its natural beauty makes it a popular destination for both local and international tourists.

Nacpan Beach

Nacpan Beach is a beautiful long stretch of white sand located in El Nido, Palawan. It is known for its clear blue water, peaceful surroundings, and scenic views. The beach is part of the famous El Nido island attractions and is a great place for swimming, relaxing, and enjoying the sunset.

Big Lagoon

Big Lagoon is one of the most famous attractions in El Nido. It is surrounded by tall limestone cliffs and has clear blue-green water. Visitors can explore the lagoon by kayak or small boat while enjoying the beautiful natural scenery.

Small Lagoon

Small Lagoon is known for its peaceful waters and dramatic limestone surroundings. Visitors can enter the lagoon through a small opening between the rocks and explore the area by swimming or kayaking.

Hidden Beach

Hidden Beach is a unique destination surrounded by limestone cliffs. The beach is hidden from the open sea and can be reached by passing through a small opening between the rocks. It is known for its beautiful scenery and clear water.

Cadlao Island

Cadlao Island is one of the largest islands in El Nido. It features beautiful beaches, limestone formations, forests, and clear waters. The island is a popular destination for island hopping, swimming, snorkeling, and enjoying nature.
